# Architecture Impact

## Use this skill when

Assess the system consequences of a proposed or approved change and update authorized architecture records, risks, debts or open questions.

## Authority and scope

The impact policy and schemas own deterministic constraints. Meaning-changing architecture, security, privacy and destructive choices require human approval. Findings remain proposed or open until that evidence exists.

## Workflow

1. Load the target, dependency graph and policy-selected narratives.
2. Examine data, API, integration, security, privacy, deployment and operational effects that are relevant to the task.
3. Separate confirmed impact from assumptions and unresolved choices.
4. Update only authorized architecture paths and atomic records.
5. Validate references and report approval requirements.

## Non-responsibilities

Do not approve architecture, sign off security, choose product priority, modify unrelated scope, or implement the design.
